# The commerce registries

[`rushing/laravel-commerce`](https://github.com/stephenr85/laravel-commerce) declares four registries
under `commerce.`. Each is a place where your application, or a package layered on top, plugs its own
vocabulary into an engine that ships deliberately neutral: the engine knows how to take a payment,
compose a bill and publish a product feed, but not what your rails, your priced components or your
catalog are.

Which one you reach for:

- **Collect money through a new payment provider**: [`commerce.money-in.drivers`](commerce.money-in.drivers).
  `fake`, `stripe` and `none` ship registered.
- **Price a line on a bill (seats, tokens, storage)**:
  [`commerce.billing.components`](commerce.billing.components). Ships empty; your application
  registers every component.
- **Turn metered usage into a charge by a rule other than a flat markup**:
  [`commerce.billing.pricing-strategies`](commerce.billing.pricing-strategies). `linear` ships
  registered.
- **Publish your catalog as an Agentic Commerce Protocol product feed**:
  [`commerce.acp.feeds`](commerce.acp.feeds). Ships empty; you register one projector per catalog
  Data class.

## How they fit together

The two billing registries work as a pair. `BillComposer` walks a Plan's component list, builds
each component from `commerce.billing.components` and asks it for a line item. A component that
charges for metered usage looks up its pricing rule in `commerce.billing.pricing-strategies` by the
name in its config. The finished bill is then collected through whichever driver
`commerce.money-in.drivers` resolves. The ACP feed registry stands apart: it is the discovery half
of agentic checkout, read when the feed is served, not when money moves.

## What a duplicate means here

All four **supersede**: registering a taken key replaces the entry. That is how you override what
ships, for example by registering your own `stripe` driver or your own `linear` strategy. The one
sharp edge is the ACP feed registry: it keys each projector by its subject class's short name, and two
subjects that share a short name throw rather than replace each other silently.
